💰 Dear 50 Jackal Prize Tiers Explained

The Dear 50 Jackal draw distributes prizes across multiple tiers so that winning is not an all-or-nothing affair:

Jackpot (1st Prize, ₹21 Lakh): A single ticket takes the headline prize each draw. The number includes a series letter — your ticket must match it exactly, series included.

Consolation: Unique to Indian state lotteries — if your number matches the jackpot digits but carries a different series letter, you still win. Always check this tier; it is the most commonly overlooked win.

Lower tiers: Drawn from shorter digit matches, which is why they produce the most winners. The smallest tier's four-digit match means a ₹50 ticket has a far better chance of winning something than the jackpot odds suggest.

Prize amounts are fixed by the Punjab lottery department and identical for every Dear 50 Jackal draw — see the prize table above for the exact amounts. Winnings up to ₹10,000 can be claimed at any authorized retailer; larger prizes require a visit to the lottery office within 30 days. TDS of 30% applies to prizes above ₹10,000.
