🏆 Claiming Your Dear 50 Beast Prize — Step by Step

  1. Verify first: Match your ticket against every prize tier in the table above, then confirm with the official PDF linked on this page. Do not sign the ticket until you are sure.
  2. Prizes up to ₹10,000: Take your original ticket to any authorized Punjab lottery retailer. You will be paid on the spot after the retailer verifies the number.
  3. Prizes above ₹10,000: Visit the Punjab lottery office (or the Directorate of State Lotteries) with your original ticket, a government ID proof (Aadhaar/PAN), passport-size photographs, and a filled prize claim form. The office verifies the ticket's authenticity before releasing payment.

  6. Keep copies: Photograph both sides of your ticket before submitting it, and get a receipt for every claim submission.
