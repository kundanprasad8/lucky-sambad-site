Consolation (₹1,000): Unique to Indian state lotteries — if your number matches the jackpot digits but carries a different series letter, you still win ₹1,000. Always check this tier; it is the most commonly overlooked win.

2nd Prize (₹9,000): Ten winning numbers per draw. This tier turns a ₹50 ticket into a serious payout.
