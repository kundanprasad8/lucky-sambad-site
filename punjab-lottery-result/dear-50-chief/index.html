💰 Dear 50 Chief Prize Tiers Explained

The Dear 50 Chief prize structure is designed so that many tickets win in every draw, not just the jackpot holder. Here is what each tier means for you:

1st Prize (₹21 Lakh): The jackpot — awarded to the single ticket matching the complete winning number including the series letter. This is the life-changing prize that makes headlines, and it is subject to 30% TDS deduction before payout.

Consolation Prize: A special tier for tickets that match the 1st prize number in every digit except the series letter. It softens the sting of a near-miss — check the prize table above for the exact consolation amount on this draw.

Lower tiers: The 2nd, 3rd, 4th and 5th prizes are drawn from shorter digit matches, which is why they produce the most winners — the smallest tier typically matches only the last four digits, so even a partial match on your ticket can return several times the ₹50 ticket cost.

Always check every tier before discarding a ticket — most unclaimed prizes are small wins players never noticed. Match your number against the full table above, then verify with the official PDF.
