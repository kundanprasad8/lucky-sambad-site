Consolation Prize (₹1,000) rewards near-misses: same digits as the jackpot, different series letter. In a typical draw, dozens of consolation prizes are paid out.

2nd Prize (₹9,000) is drawn as a separate set of about ten numbers. Do not assume your ticket lost just because it missed the jackpot — always scan this tier.

3rd Prize (₹450): A 75x return on a ₹6 ticket. Dozens of winners per draw.

4th Prize (₹250) and 5th Prize (₹120): The volume tiers — hundreds of winning numbers drawn from partial digit matches. Most Dear 50 Colt winners each draw come from these two tiers.

Because the lower tiers match fewer digits, your odds of winning something are far better than the jackpot odds suggest. Check every tier in the table above before you throw a ticket away — unclaimed small prizes are the most common lottery regret.
