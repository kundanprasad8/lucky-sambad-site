🏆 How to Claim Your Nagaland Lottery Prize

Won a Nagaland lottery prize? Act quickly — every prize must be claimed within 30 days of the draw date. Here is the exact process:

  1. Verify your win: Match your ticket against every prize tier in the result table above, then confirm with the official PDF linked on the draw page. Do not sign the ticket until you are sure.
  2. Prizes up to ₹10,000: Take your original ticket to any authorized lottery retailer — you are paid on the spot after the retailer verifies the number.
  3. Prizes above ₹10,000: Visit the Nagaland lottery office (Directorate of State Lotteries) with your original ticket and the documents listed below.
  4. Submit and take a receipt: Hand over the ticket and documents, and always collect a stamped receipt. Photograph both sides of your ticket before submitting it.
  5. Collect your payout: Once the office verifies the ticket's authenticity, the prize is released minus TDS (see the tax note below).

⏰ 30-Day Claim Deadline

All Nagaland lottery prizes must be claimed within 30 days of the draw date. Unclaimed prizes lapse and are forfeited to the state — the deadline is strict, with no extensions. Mark the date as soon as you discover a win.

💸 Tax on Lottery Winnings

Winnings above ₹10,000 attract 30% TDS (plus applicable cess) deducted at source before payout. Lottery income is taxed as "income from other sources" — declare the gross amount in your Income Tax Return and keep the TDS certificate (Form 16A) the lottery office issues.
