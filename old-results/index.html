❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How can I check a Lottery Sambad old result?

Type the draw name (for example Karunya Plus or Dear Morning) or your ticket number in the search box above — past winning numbers appear instantly with the date. Or pick the draw date from the calendar and open that day's full results page.

How far back does this old results archive go?

This archive currently holds results from 09 October 2026 onwards and grows every day. Every date's page carries the full prize lists for all draws declared that day, exactly as published.

Can I still claim a prize from an old draw?

Yes — prizes can be claimed within 30 days of the draw date. Keep your original ticket safe, verify the number here and against the official PDF, then claim at the state lottery office or an authorised claim centre with your ID proof.

Why is today's result not in this archive?

Today's results are published live on the homepage as each draw is declared. Once the day ends, the full day moves into this archive — so yesterday is always the newest date here.
