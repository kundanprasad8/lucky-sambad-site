⚖️ Are Lotteries Legal in India?

Yes — The state lotteries operate legally. Lotteries are permitted in 13 Indian states under the Lotteries (Regulation) Act, 1998, and every draw on this page is run by a state government: draws use mechanical draw machines under official supervision, and results are published as official PDFs. Tickets are physical paper tickets sold through licensed retailers only, and you must be 18 or older to buy one. If you live in a state where lotteries are banned, do not purchase tickets. 🎲 Play Responsibly

  • Lottery is entertainment, not income — never spend money you cannot afford to lose.
  • Set a budget before you buy a ticket and stick to it; never chase losses.
  • Every ticket has identical odds — past results do not change future draws.
  • Ignore anyone promising "guaranteed" winning numbers — it is always a scam.
  • If playing stops being fun, talk to someone you trust and seek help.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How can I download the lottery result PDF?

Find your draw and date in the table on this page and tap 'Official PDF' — the file opens directly from the official state lottery board's website. Every draw page on Lucky Sambad also carries a PDF download button for its latest official result.

Are these PDFs official?

Yes. Only results verified against an official source are listed here, and every link points to the official PDF hosted by the state lottery board itself (for example Nagaland, Kerala, Punjab and Arunachal Pradesh). Results from secondary sources are never listed as PDFs.

Why is my draw's PDF not in the list?

Some draws are published from secondary sources first, and a few boards do not release a public PDF for every draw. The list grows automatically as official PDFs are fetched and verified — check the draw's own page for its latest status.

Can I claim a prize using a downloaded PDF?

The official PDF is the document accepted for claim verification — match your ticket number against it carefully. The claim itself must be made in person with your original ticket at a retailer (up to ₹10,000) or the state lottery office (above ₹10,000) within 30 days.

Is downloading the result PDF free?

Yes — official result PDFs are public documents and completely free to download. Never pay anyone for a result PDF or for 'winning number' information.
