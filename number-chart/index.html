❓ Number Chart FAQs

What are hot lottery numbers?

Hot numbers are the first-prize winning numbers that have appeared most often in the draws tracked on this page. The chart above recomputes them daily from every published result — they describe what has happened, not what will happen.

Do hot or cold numbers increase my chances of winning?

No. Every lottery draw is independent and random — past results do not change the odds of any ticket. This chart is for information and curiosity only, never a prediction or betting tip.

How often is the number chart updated?

The chart is recomputed automatically every day when new results are published, and always covers every result file from the tracking start date shown at the top of this page.

Which draws are included in the number chart?

All published draws across all 13 states covered by Lucky Sambad — including the 1 PM, 6 PM and 8 PM Nagaland Dear draws plus every state-specific morning, afternoon, evening and night draw.
