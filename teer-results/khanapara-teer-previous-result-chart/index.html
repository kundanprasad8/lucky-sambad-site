❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Khanapara Teer Previous Result Chart?

The Khanapara Teer Previous Result Chart is the complete date-wise history of first-round (FR) and second-round (SR) winning numbers for Khanapara Teer and Khanapara Morning Teer, organised by month. It is built from results published on Lucky Sambad.

How far back does the chart go?

The chart starts from the first results in Lucky Sambad's own archive (October 2026) and grows every day. We never backfill with unverified third-party history.

What do FR and SR mean in Teer?

FR is the First Round and SR is the Second Round. In each round, archers shoot arrows at a target and the last two digits of the total arrows hitting the target become the winning number (00–99).

Can previous Teer results predict the next number?

No. Every round is an independent physical arrow count — past results do not change the odds of any future round. Anyone selling 'sure' or 'guaranteed' Teer numbers is running a scam.
