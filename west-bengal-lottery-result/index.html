💰 West Bengal Lottery Prize Structure

West Bengal draws typically offer five prize tiers. The 1st prize (jackpot) goes up to ₹1 Crore, followed by smaller consolation prizes — commonly ₹9,000 (2nd), ₹450 (3rd), ₹250 (4th) and ₹120 (5th). Exact amounts differ by draw and are shown on each draw page.

🏆 How to Claim Your Prize

  1. Keep your original ticket safe — photocopies and photos are not accepted for claims.
  2. Prizes up to ₹10,000 can be claimed from any authorized lottery retailer.
  3. For prizes above ₹10,000, visit the state lottery office with your ticket, ID proof and a filled claim form.
  4. You must claim within 30 days of the draw date — unclaimed prizes lapse.
  5. Winnings above ₹10,000 are subject to TDS (tax deducted at source) as per Indian tax rules.
