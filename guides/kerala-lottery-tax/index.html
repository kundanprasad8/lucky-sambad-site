Kerala Lottery Tax — Kitna Tax Lagega

Seedha Jawab: Kerala lottery jeetne par 30% income tax + 4% health & education cess lagta hai — kul 31.2%. ₹10,000 se zyada ke inaam par ye TDS ke roop me source par hi kat jata hai. Koi chhoot ya katoti nahi milti.

Kerala (केरल) lottery me inaam jeetna khushi ki baat hai — par tax ka hisaab pehle se samajh lena samajhdari hai. Bharat me lottery ki jeet par Income Tax Act ke Section 115BB ke tehat flat 30% tax + 4% health & education cess lagta hai. Kul milakar 31.2% rakam tax me chali jati hai. Ye niyam pure Bharat me ek jaisa hai — chahe aap Kerala ki lottery khele ya kisi aur rajya ki.

TDS Kab Aur Kaise Kat-ta Hai?

Section 194B ke tehat ₹10,000 se zyada ke inaam par 30% TDS (cess sahit 31.2%) payment se pehle hi kaat liya jata hai. Matlab:

  • ₹1 crore jeetne par aapko ₹68.8 lakh milenge — ₹31.2 lakh pehle kat jayega
  • Katne wala (lottery vibhag) aapko Form 16A (TDS certificate) dega
  • Ye TDS aapke PAN se link hota hai — isliye bade claim me PAN anivarya hai
  • Agar PAN na ho to zyada TDS (20% extra nahi — 194B me fixed) kat sakta hai; PAN zaroor rakhein

Kya Lottery Jeet Par Koi Chhoot Milti Hai?

Nahi. Section 115BB ke tehat:

  • Koi katoti (deduction) nahi — ticket ki keemat bhi nahi ghata sakte
  • Koi basic exemption nahi — pehle rupaye se tax
  • Kisi aur nuksan se set-off nahi kar sakte
  • Ye aay special rate se taxable hai, slab me nahi judti

ITR Me Lottery Jeet Kaise Dikhayein?

  1. ITR form me "Income from Other Sources" me dikhayein
  2. Schedule OS me lottery income alag se mention karein
  3. TDS (Form 16A) ka credit lein — zyada kata ho to refund milega
  4. Bade inaam par CA ki salah zaroor lein

Advance Tax Aur ITR — Poori Prakriya

TDS katne ke baad bhi aapko ITR me lottery income dikhana anivarya hai:

  1. Form 16A sambhalein: Ye TDS ka saboot hai — ITR me TDS credit isi se milega
  2. Form 26AS/AIS check karein: Income tax portal par dekhein ki TDS aapke PAN me dikh raha hai
  3. Schedule OS bharein: "Income from Other Sources" me lottery income, Section 115BB rate se
  4. TDS credit lein: Kata hua TDS adjust hoga; zyada kata ho to refund milega
  5. Deadline: Aam taur par 31 July (vyakti) — der par jurmana lagta hai

Tax Ke Liye Record Kaise Rakhein?

  • Jeetne wale ticket ki copy (photo/scan)
  • Claim receipt aur acknowledgement number
  • Form 16A (TDS certificate)
  • Bank statement — payment credit ka proof
  • CA ki salah ka written record (bade inaam par)

Ye documents kam se kam 6–8 saal tak sambhal kar rakhein — income tax scrutiny me kaam aayenge.
