1 Crore Lottery Jeetne Par Haath Me Kitna Milega?

₹1 crore ki lottery jeetne par haath me lagbhag ₹69-70 lakh aata hai. Aisa isliye hai kyunki ₹10,000 se zyada ki winnings par 30% TDS (Section 194B) kaat liya jata hai — matlab ₹30 lakh seedha tax me jata hai — aur uske upar badi winnings par surcharge aur 4% health aur education cess bhi lag sakta hai. Bahut se log ₹1 crore sunte hi poori rashi ka plan bana lete hain, lekin haqiqat me tax ke baad jo rashi milti hai wo lagbhag 30% kam hoti hai.

TDS kab aur kaise kat ta hai

TDS aapko prize milne se pehle hi kaat liya jata hai. Matlab lottery department ya distributor aapko ₹1 crore ka cheque nahi dega — wo ₹30 lakh (plus applicable cess/surcharge) kaat kar sarkar ko jama karega aur baaki rashi aapke bank account me transfer karega. Ye process Section 194B ke tahat automatic hai; aapko alag se tax jama nahi karna padta, lekin ITR me is winnings ko declare zaroor karna hota hai taaki kata hua TDS aapke naam credit ho sake.

Nagaland vs Kerala — ₹1 crore kahan milta hai

Nagaland Nagaland Dear Lottery me 1st prize fixed ₹1 crore hai (₹6 ticket par), isliye ye calculation wahan ke winners par seedha lagu hoti hai. Kerala Kerala me weekly schemes ka 1st prize aam taur par ₹70-80 lakh tak hota hai — us par bhi 30% TDS katega, to haath me lagbhag ₹49-56 lakh aayega. Kerala ke bumper draws me jab 1st prize crores me hota hai, tab bhi yehi 30% wala niyam lagu hota hai.

Bade prize par samajhdari se faisle lein

₹69-70 lakh ek badi rashi hai, aur achanak itna paisa milne par galat faisle bhi aasani se ho jate hain. Pehle tax advisor se ITR declaration samajh lein, phir kisi financial advisor se baat karke rashi ka plan banayein. Prize ki rashi bank transfer se aati hai, isliye apne bank account details (aur cancelled cheque) claim ke waqt sahi-sahi dein — galat details se payment atak sakta hai.

₹1 crore jeetne wale kai log ye galti karte hain ki wo ghoshit rashi ko hi apna budget maan lete hain. Haqiqat me aapke haath me lagbhag ₹69-70 lakh aayega — isliye koi bhi bada kharcha (ghar, gaadi, nivesh) isi net rashi par plan karein. Ek aur zaroori baat: TDS katne ke baad bhi aapko ITR me poori ₹1 crore ki winnings declare karni hai, aur kata hua ₹30 lakh ka TDS credit ke roop me adjust hoga. Ye process samajhne ke liye hamara TDS wala FAQ padhein.

Practical tip: ₹1 crore jeetne ki khabar turant sabko na batayein. Pehle ticket surakshit karein, 30 din ke andar claim file karein, aur tax advisor se salah lekar hi aage ka plan banayein.

Kya ₹1 crore par poora tax dena padta hai?

Haan, 30% TDS ke saath surcharge aur 4% cess bhi lag sakta hai; haath me lagbhag ₹69-70 lakh aata hai.

TDS ka paisa wapas mil sakta hai?

Kata hua TDS aapke PAN par credit hota hai aur ITR me adjust hota hai; zyada kata ho to refund claim ho sakta hai.

₹70 lakh jeetne par kitna katega?

30% ke hisaab se lagbhag ₹21 lakh TDS, plus applicable cess/surcharge — haath me lagbhag ₹48-49 lakh.

Prize cash me mil sakta hai?

Bade prizes aam taur par bank transfer/cheque se milte hain; cash payment ki ummeed na rakhein.

1 crore jeetne par ITR me kaise dikhaye?

'Income from Other Sources' me declare karein; kata hua TDS credit me adjust ho jayega.
