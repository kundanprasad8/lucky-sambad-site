Lottery Ticket Kho Jaye To Prize Claim Ho Sakta Hai?

Nahi — lottery ticket kho jane par prize claim generally nahi hota, kyunki claim ke liye original winning ticket jama karna mandatory hai. Photocopy, photo ya yaad-dasht par bataya hua number claim ke liye kaafi nahi hota. Ticket hi aapki jeet ka ekmatra saboot hai, isliye use kharidte hi surakshit jagah rakhna sabse zaroori aadat hai. Ticket milte hi uske peeche sign kar dein aur dono taraf ki saaf photo le lein — photo claim to nahi dilayegi, lekin record ke liye kaam aayegi.

Lottery system bearer-document par chalta hai — matlab jiske paas original ticket hai, wahi winner mana jata hai. Ticket khone se bachne ke upay

Ticket kharidte hi uske peeche apna naam aur sign kar dein — isse agar ticket kahin gir bhi jaye to koi aur aasani se us par daava nahi kar payega. Phir uski dono taraf se saaf photo le lein aur original ko kisi file, wallet ya locker me rakhein — jeb me ghumana ya kitaabon me dabana risky hai. Ghar me ek fixed jagah tay kar lein jahan saare lottery tickets rakhe jayenge. Ticket milne par turant kya karein

Result me apna number dekhte hi pehle ticket ko surakshit karein, phir claim ki taiyaari karein. Ticket ko paani, dhoop aur zyada modne se bachayein — kharab ticket ka claim bhi mushkil ho jata hai. Draw date se 30 din ke andar claim karna hai, isliye photo lene aur file me rakhne ke baad claim form ki taiyaari shuru kar dein. Manipur Manipur ke players ke liye bhi yehi basic suraksha niyam lagu hote hain.

Photocopy ya photo ka kya fayda

Ye samajhna zaroori hai ki ticket ki photo claim nahi dilayegi, lekin bekaar bhi nahi hai. Agar ticket kho jaye to photo se aapko ticket number, scheme aur draw date yaad rahegi — aur agar ticket kahin ghar me hi misplaced hua hai to dhoondhne me madad milegi. Kuch log photo ko police complaint ya affidavit ke saath jodne ki sochte hain, lekin lottery niyamon me bina original ticket ke claim ka pravdhan generally nahi hai.

Practical tip: Aaj hi ek chhoti file ya envelope lein aur us par 'Lottery Tickets' likhkar ek fixed jagah rakh dein — har naya ticket seedha usi me jayega. Ye chhoti aadat bade nuksaan se bachati hai.
