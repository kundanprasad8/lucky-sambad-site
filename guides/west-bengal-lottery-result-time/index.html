Result Check Karte Waqt Kya Milayein?

Result Me Der Ho To Kya Karein?

Kabhi-kabhi takniki karanon se result 30–60 minute late ho sakta hai. Ghabrayein nahi:

  • Page refresh karke dobara check karein
  • Official site (lotterysambad.com) par dekhein
  • 2–3 ghante se zyada der ho to agle din akhbaar me confirm karein
  • "Result leak" ya "pehle se result" ka daava karne walon se bachein — ye thagi hai

Purani draw dates ke results West Bengal result archive me milte hain. Ticket ka draw nikalne ke 30 din ke andar claim karna zaroori hai — isliye purana ticket hai to turant check karein! Claim process yahan padhein.

Ticket Sale Kab Band Hoti Hai?

Har draw se lagbhag 30 minute pehle ticket sale band ho jati hai. Iske baad kharida gaya ticket agle draw ka hoga — dhokhe me na rahein. Retailer se ticket lete waqt draw date zaroor check karein:

  • Aaj ke draw ka ticket = aaj ki date + sahi draw time
  • Kal ke draw ka ticket = kal ki date (advance me bhi mil sakta hai)
  • Purani date ka ticket = bekar, bilkul na lein
