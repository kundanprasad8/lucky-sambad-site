Lottery Sambad 1PM Ka Result Kab Aata Hai?

Last Updated: October 2026

Lottery Sambad ka 1PM Morning draw ka result lagbhag 1:00 PM par declare hota hai, aur official result PDF usually 1:05 PM se 1:15 PM ke beech lotterysambad.com par upload ho jata hai. Ye Nagaland State Lotteries ka pehla daily draw hai — ticket sirf ₹6 ka hota hai aur first prize ₹1 crore ka hota hai.

Related Jankari

1PM Morning Draw Ka Poora Schedule

Nagaland State Lotteries roz teen draw nikalta hai — subah 1:00 PM (Morning), shaam 6:00 PM (Day) aur raat 8:00 PM (Night). Teenon draws ke ticket alag-alag hote hain aur har draw ka apna result PDF banta hai. 1PM wala draw din ka pehla draw hota hai, isliye iske ticket ki bikri usually draw se kuch ghante pehle band ho jati hai. Draw machine se nikalne ke baad result ko verify karke PDF banaya jata hai, phir use official website par publish kiya jata hai. Isi process ki wajah se website par PDF aane me 5 se 15 minute ka waqt lag sakta hai — iska matlab ye nahi ki result me deri ho rahi hai, ye normal process hai.

Result Kab Aur Kahan Check Karein

Sabse reliable source official website lotterysambad.com hai, jahan har draw ka result PDF date-wise upload hota hai. Result check karte waqt hamesha date aur draw time (Morning/Day/Night) match karein — aksar log galat draw ka PDF khol lete hain aur confuse ho jate hain. PDF me apna ticket number poori tarah match karein, kyunki prize sirf exact match par milta hai. Agar aap Nagaland ke bahar se result dekh rahe hain to yaad rakhein ki ye result sirf jankari ke liye hai — ticket khareedna ya prize claim karna sirf authorized states me, authorized retailers se hi valid hai.

Prize Jeetne Par Kya Karein

Agar aapka ticket 1PM draw me prize jeet jata hai to sabse pehle ticket ko sign karke surakshit jagah rakhein — bina sign kiya ticket kho jane par claim mushkil ho sakta hai. Prize claim karne ke liye aapke paas 30 din ka samay hota hai draw ki date se. Claim ke liye original signed ticket, claim form, ID proof, address proof, PAN card, passport-size photos aur bank details chahiye hote hain. ₹10,000 se upar ki jeet par 30% TDS (Section 194B) kat-ta hai, isliye net amount usi hisab se milega. Nagaland State Lotteries ka office Kohima me hai, jahan bade prize ke claims process hote hain.

1PM draw un logon me popular hai jo dopahar me hi result dekhna chahte hain. Draw ka naam din ke hisab se badal sakta hai, lekin timing roz same rehti hai — Sunday ko bhi. Teenon draws ka result ek hi PDF me nahi aata — har draw ka alag PDF hota hai, isliye Morning wala PDF hi kholein. ❓ Aksar Puche Jaane Wale Sawal

Lottery Sambad 1PM ka result PDF kitne baje upload hota hai?

Usually 1:05 PM se 1:15 PM ke beech lotterysambad.com par Morning draw ka PDF upload ho jata hai. Kabhi-kabhi technical wajah se thodi deri ho sakti hai.

Kya 1PM draw roz hota hai, Sunday ko bhi?

Haan, Nagaland ke teenon draws (1PM, 6PM, 8PM) roz hote hain — Sunday ko bhi koi chhutti nahi hoti, timing same rehti hai.

1PM draw ke ticket ki keemat kitni hai?

Dear Morning draw ka ticket ₹6 ka hota hai, aur iska first prize ₹1 crore ka hota hai.

1PM aur 6PM draw ka ticket same hota hai kya?

Nahi, teenon draws ke ticket alag-alag hote hain. 1PM draw me sirf Morning wala ticket valid hai — Day ya Night wala ticket usme kaam nahi karega.

1PM draw me prize jeetne par claim kaise karein?

Ticket par sign karke 30 din ke andar claim karein. Original ticket, claim form, ID proof, address proof, PAN, photos aur bank details lagenge; ₹10,000 se upar par 30% TDS katega.
