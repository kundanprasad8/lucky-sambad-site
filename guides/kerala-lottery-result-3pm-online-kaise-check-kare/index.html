Result Ke Baad Claim Ki Tayyari

Agar aapka number lag gaya hai to badhai ho — lekin ab turant action lein. Kerala me prize result ke 30 din ke andar claim karna hota hai. Chhote prizes authorized retailer se mil jate hain, jabki bade prizes Directorate of State Lotteries, Thiruvananthapuram me claim hote hain. Claim ke liye signed original ticket, claim form, ID proof, PAN aur bank details chahiye honge. Poori claim process kerala-lottery-prize-claim-kaise-kare page par step-by-step samjhayi gayi hai.
