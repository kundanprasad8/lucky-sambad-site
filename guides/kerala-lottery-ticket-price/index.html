Kerala Lottery Ticket Price List

Seedha Jawab: Kerala lottery ticket price ₹40 se ₹300 tak hai (MRP). Har scheme ka alag price hota hai — neeche poori scheme-wise list dekhein. Bumper draws me ticket thoda mehenga hota hai.

Kerala (केरल) lottery ticket ka price har scheme ke hisaab se tay hota hai. Kerala Lotteries Department har ticket par MRP (Maximum Retail Price) print karta hai — retailer isse zyada paise nahi le sakta. Kul milakar yahan ticket ₹40 se ₹300 tak me milta hai, aur weekly draws me ₹1 crore tak, bumper draws me ₹10 crore se bhi zyada tak ka inaam jeeta ja sakta hai.

Ticket Par Kya-Kya Printed Hota Hai?

Har asli ticket par ye details zaroor honi chahiye:

  • Series aur ticket number — jaise "AB 123456"
  • Draw ka naam aur date — kab draw niklega
  • MRP — zyada paise dene se inkar karein
  • Security features — hologram, barcode ya watermark
  • Prize structure — chhote aksharon me peeche ki taraf

MRP Se Zyada Paise Mange To Kya Karein?

Ye galat hai. Retailer MRP se ek rupaya zyada nahi le sakta. Agar koi zyada mange to:

  1. Paise dene se inkar karein aur dusre retailer se lein
  2. Retailer ka naam/dukkan note karein
  3. Kerala Lotteries Department (keralalotteries.com) me shikayat darj karayein

Budget Me Kaise Khelein? (Samajhdari Ke Tips)

  • Monthly budget tay karein — jaise mahine me ₹200–₹500 se zyada nahi
  • Chhote ticket se shuru karein — pehle weekly draws try karein
  • Bumper ke liye alag rakhein — bada ticket lene se pehle soch lein
  • Udhar lekar kabhi na khelein — lottery manoranjan hai, nivesh nahi
  • Har ticket ka hisaab rakhein — kharcha kabhi budget se bahar na jaye

Ticket Kharidte Waqt Checklist

  • Ticket par draw date aane wali hai (purani date ka ticket bekar hai)
  • Series-number saaf aur poora printed hai
  • Ticket kata-phata ya mita hua nahi hai
  • MRP ticket par likhi keemat se match kar rahi hai
  • Retailer authorized hai (agents guide dekhein)

Ticket kharidne ka poora tarika kaise khele guide me aur result timing yahan dekhein.

Ticket Series System Kya Hai?

Har lottery ticket ka number do hisson me hota hai — series (jaise AB, CD) aur number (jaise 123456). Result me dono match hone chahiye. Kuch schemes me series-wise prize bhi hota hai — matlab poori series par inaam lag sakta hai. Isliye ticket lete waqt series bhi note kar lein, sirf number nahi.

  • Series: Ticket ke shuru ke 1–2 akshar — print run ki pehchan
  • Number: 5–6 ank ka unique number
  • Dono milna zaroori: Sirf number milna kaafi nahi, series bhi match honi chahiye

Har Retailer Par Price Same Kyun Hota Hai?

Kyunki MRP sarkar tay karti hai, retailer nahi. Kerala Lotteries Department har print run ka MRP fix karta hai aur ticket par print karata hai. Iska matlab:

  • Mumbai ho ya chhota kasba — keemat ek hi
  • Koi "wholesale rate" ya "discount" nahi hota
  • Jo kam me de, wo nakli ticket de raha hai — savdhaan!
  • Jo zyada mange, wo niyam tod raha hai — shikayat karein

Ticket Price Se Jude Aam Sawal

  • Kya ticket par discount milta hai? — Nahi. MRP fixed hai; "discount" ke naam par nakli ticket mil sakta hai.
  • Bulk me ticket saste milte hain? — Nahi. Har ticket ki keemat MRP hi hai, chahe 1 lo ya 100.
  • Ticket par GST alag se lagta hai? — Nahi. MRP me sab kuch shamil hai; retailer alag se GST nahi maang sakta.
