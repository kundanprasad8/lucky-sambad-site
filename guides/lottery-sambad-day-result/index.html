"Evening Result" Ka Confusion Clear Karein

Bahut se log "evening lottery result" search karte hain. Dhyan rahe:

  • 6 PM draw ko kabhi-kabhi "evening" bhi keh dete hain, lekin official naam "Day" hai.
  • 8 PM draw ka official naam "Evening/Night" hai.
  • Confusion se bachne ke liye ticket par likha time (18:00 ya 20:00) dekhein.

Day Draw Ticket Kab Aur Kahan Se Kharidein?

  • Ticket dopahar se shaam tak authorized retailers par milte hain.
  • 6 PM se pehle kharidna zaroori hai — draw ke baad ticket nahi milta.
  • Ticket par date, time (18:00) aur number saaf printed hona chahiye.
  • Online/WhatsApp par "6 PM draw ticket" bechne wale fraud hain — bachein.

Jeetne Par Kya Karein?

  • Original ticket sambhal kar rakhein — photo backup lein.
  • ₹10,000 tak: retailer/stockist se claim karein.
  • Usse upar: lottery department me claim form + ID proof ke saath.
  • 30% TDS (+ cess) ₹10,000 se upar ke prizes par katega.
  • 30 din ki claim time limit ka dhyan rakhein.

Sources: Nagaland State Lotteries official results, Lotteries (Regulation) Act 1998.

Day Draw Me Fraud Se Kaise Bachein?

  • Nakli ticket: Shaam ki bheed me nakli ticket bechne wale sakriya hote hain. Print quality aur government markings check karein.
  • "Result pehle bata dunga": Koi bhi result se pehle winning number nahi janta. Aisa daava karne wala fraud hai.
  • Online ticket: WhatsApp/Telegram par "Day draw ticket" bechne wale illegal hain.
  • Prize ke naam par paise mangna: "Aapka prize laga hai, pehle tax bhejo" — ye classic fraud hai. Real lottery me prize se pehle koi paise nahi mangta.
