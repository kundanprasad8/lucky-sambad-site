Dear Lottery Me 2nd Prize Kitna Milta Hai?

Last Updated: October 2026

Nagaland Dear Lottery me 2nd prize ₹9,000 milta hai. Ye rashi fixed hai aur Dear Morning, Day aur Evening — teeno rozana draws me same rehti hai. Chunki ye rashi ₹10,000 se kam hai, isliye is par 30% TDS aam taur par nahi kat ta, aur jeetne wale ko poore ₹9,000 milte hain. Phir bhi result milte hi ticket ke peeche sign karke use surakshit rakhna zaroori hai, kyunki claim ke liye original ticket chahiye hota hai.

2nd prize par tax kyon nahi kat ta

Income Tax Act ke Section 194B ke tahat lottery winnings par 30% TDS tabhi kaata jata hai jab jeet ki rashi ₹10,000 se zyada ho. Chunki 2nd prize ₹9,000 hai, jo is seema se kam hai, isliye is par TDS deduction aam taur par nahi hota. Lekin dhyaan rahe — winner ko apni winnings Income Tax Return (ITR) me declare karna chahiye, chahe TDS kata ho ya nahi. Ye best practice hai aur tax niyamon ke anukool bhi hai.

2nd prize ka claim kaise karein

2nd prize jaise chhote prize aam taur par authorized lottery retailer se hi claim ho jate hain — aapko lottery office tak jaane ki zaroorat aam taur par nahi padti. Apna original ticket lekar retailer ke paas jayein, ticket ka number result se milwayein, aur retailer verification ke baad aapko rashi de dega. Ticket ke peeche pehle se sign kar dena achhi aadat hai. [[STATE:west_bengal]] West Bengal me bhi Dear Lottery ke tickets bikte hain, aur wahan bhi chhote prizes retailer ke through claim hote hain — apne state ke official notification me claim process zaroor check karein.

Claim ki 30 din ki deadline 2nd prize par bhi lagu hoti hai. Matlab draw ki date se 30 din ke andar prize claim kar lena chahiye, warna prize lapse ho sakta hai. Isliye result aate hi der na karein.

Ek se zyada ticket par 2nd prize lage to

Agar aapke ek se zyada tickets par prize laga hai, to har ticket ka claim alag-alag hota hai. Har jeetne wale ticket ko alag se verify karwayein. Chhoti rashiyon par TDS ka chakkar nahi hota, lekin saari winnings ka hisaab ITR me dena sahi rehta hai. Punjab Punjab Lottery ke results bhi hamari site par uplabdh hain — wahan ke prize structure ke liye official notification dekhein.

Result sheet me apna number milate waqt dhyaan rakhein ki Dear Lottery ke teeno draws (Morning, Day, Evening) ki alag-alag result sheets hoti hain — apne ticket ki draw timing se sahi sheet kholein. Galat sheet me number milana bekaar hai. 2nd prize ka number milte hi ticket ko alag envelope me daal dein taaki dusre tickets se mix na ho, aur usi din ya agle din retailer se claim kar lein.

Practical tip: ₹9,000 ka prize milte hi uska kharcha-planning na karein — pehle ticket ka claim poora kar lein, kyunki bina original ticket ke koi prize nahi milta, chahe rashi chhoti ho ya badi.
