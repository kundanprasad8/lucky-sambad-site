Bodoland Lottery Ticket Price Kitna Hai?

Bodoland lottery ticket ki keemat usually lagbhag ₹6 hoti hai — ye ticket par printed MRP hai aur authorized retailer ko isi daam par bechna chahiye. Ticket par face value, draw naam, date aur number printed hota hai. MRP se zyada mange to inkar karein; online bikri prohibited hai.

Ticket ki keemat official notification se badal sakti hai — isliye hamesha ticket par printed MRP ko final manein. Special bumper draws me price zyada ho sakta hai, lekin uski suchna pehle se official taur par di jati hai. Roz teen draws hote hain, isliye teenon ke ticket ka kharch bhi pehle se budget me rakhein.

Asli ticket ki pehchan

Asli Bodoland ticket par printing saaf hoti hai — draw ka naam, date, ticket number, series aur MRP sab clearly dikhna chahiye. Haath se likha, photocopy ya bina series wala ticket kabhi na lein; aise ticket par prize claim nahi hota. Ticket lete hi uski photo phone me le lein — ye backup ke kaam aati hai, lekin claim ke liye original ticket hi chahiye hota hai.

Overcharging se kaise bachein

"Service charge", "result fee" ya "booking charge" ke naam par extra paise mangna galat hai — ticket ki keemat sirf printed MRP hai. Kuch retailers bheed ya last-minute demand ka fayda uthakar zyada daam mangte hain; aise me dusre authorized retailer se ticket lein. Aur yaad rakhein: MRP se kam daam par milne wala "sasta ticket" aksar nakli hota hai — discount ke chakkar me na padein.

Budget aur zimmedari se kheliye

Lottery manoranjan hai, kamai ka zariya nahi — pehle se ek chhota budget tay karein aur usse zyada kabhi kharch na karein. Teen draws ka matlab teen chances zaroor hai ([[assam:bodoland-lottery-timing-kya-hai]]), lekin har draw me khelna zaroori nahi. Koi bhi vyakti jo "pakka number" ya "guaranteed prize" ka daava kare, wo dhokhebaaz hai — aise logon se door rahein aur kabhi advance payment na karein.

Ticket kho jaye ya kharab ho jaye to

Ticket kho jane par prize claim karna lagbhag namumkin hai, kyunki claim ke liye original ticket hi chahiye hota hai — isliye ticket milte hi uski photo le lena sabse achhi aadat hai. Photo se claim to nahi hoga, lekin ticket number aur draw details ka record rahega. Ticket ko wallet me aise rakhein ki wo mude ya gile na ho.

Agar ticket thoda phat jaye lekin number aur details saaf dikh rahe hon to retailer se salah lein — kai baar aise ticket par bhi claim ho jata hai. Lekin poori tarah kharab ya adhjal ticket par claim mushkil hai. Isliye ticket ko aag, paani aur tez dhoop se door rakhein.

Practical tip: Ek hi trusted retailer se niyamit ticket lein — purana sambandh hone par overcharging ki sambhavna kam hoti hai aur result/claim me bhi wo madad kar deta hai. Padosi rajya ke rates ke liye [[arunachal_pradesh:arunachal-lottery-ticket-price-kitna-hai]] par bhi nazar daal sakte hain.
