Ek Se Zyada Ticket Par Prize Lage To Kya Hota Hai?

Ek se zyada tickets par prize lagne par har ticket ka claim alag-alag hota hai, aur har jeet par tax/TDS ke niyam alag se lagu hote hain. Matlab agar aapke do tickets jeete hain — ek par ₹1 crore aur ek par ₹9,000 — to ₹1 crore wale par 30% TDS katega aur ₹9,000 wale par nahi, kyunki TDS ki ₹10,000 wali seema har jeet par alag se dekhi jati hai. Saari winnings ko ITR me declare karna hota hai.

Lottery me har ticket ek independent entry hai. Isliye multiple jeetne wale tickets ko ek saath jodkar ek bada claim nahi banaya jata — har ticket apne number, apni scheme aur apni draw date ke saath alag se verify hota hai. Neeche ek example se samjhiye.

TDS har jeet par alag kyon

Section 194B ki ₹10,000 wali seema har payment par lagu hoti hai, kul jod par nahi. Isliye ₹9,000 wala ticket TDS se bachta hai chahe aapne usi din ₹1 crore bhi jeeta ho. Ye niyam winner ke favour me hai — chhoti jeet poori milti hai. Lekin ITR me dono winnings declare karni hongi, kyunki tax department ke liye aapki kul lottery income mayne rakhti hai.

Multiple tickets ka claim process

Har jeetne wale ticket ke peeche alag se sign karein aur har ticket ke liye alag claim entry banayein. Chhote prize wala ticket retailer se turant claim ho jayega, jabki bade prize wale ke liye formal claim form, PAN, bank details aur documents chahiye honge. Dono claims ki receipt alag-alag sambhal kar rakhein. Deadline dono par same hai — draw date se 30 din. Nagaland Nagaland Dear Lottery rozana teen draws nikalti hai, isliye alag-alag draws ke tickets ki dates milana na bhoolein.

Ek hi number ke multiple tickets

Kuch log ek hi lucky number ke multiple tickets kharidte hain. Agar wahi number draw me aata hai, to har ticket par utna hi prize lagta hai — jaise number par 5th prize (₹120) aaya aur aapke paas us number ke 10 tickets hain, to 10 × ₹120 = ₹1,200 milega. Har ticket ka claim alag hoga, lekin retailer ye saare ek saath verify karke de sakta hai. Punjab Punjab Lottery me bhi multiple tickets ka yehi usool hai — har ticket independent entry hai.

Multiple tickets kharidne walon ke liye ek zaroori salah: har ticket kharidte hi us par sign karke alag-alag date-wise file me rakhein. Jab result aaye to har ticket ko uski draw date ki result sheet se alag se milayein — ek hi din ke teeno Dear draws (Morning, Day, Evening) ki sheets alag hoti hain. Jeetne wale tickets ko turant alag envelope me daal dein taaki claim ke waqt koi ticket miss na ho.

Practical tip: Multiple jeetne wale tickets ko alag-alag envelope me date likhkar rakhein — claim ke waqt gadbad nahi hogi aur koi ticket chhootega nahi.

❓ Aksar Puche Jaane Wale Sawal

Dono tickets ka TDS jodkar katega?

Nahi, TDS har jeet par alag seema (₹10,000) ke hisaab se kat ta hai, kul jod par nahi.

Ek form me dono claim kar sakte hain?

Har ticket ki entry alag hoti hai; office ke nirdesh ke hisaab se alag-alag claim karein.

Same number ke 5 tickets jeete to?

Har ticket par poora prize milega — 5 tickets par 5 guna rashi, har ticket ka alag claim.

ITR me dono winnings dikhani hongi?

Haan, saari lottery winnings 'Income from Other Sources' me declare karni hoti hain.

Chhote-bade prize ek saath claim honge?

Chhota retailer se turant, bada formal process se — dono ka rasta alag hai.
