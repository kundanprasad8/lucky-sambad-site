Maharashtra Lottery Draw Timing Kya Hai?

Last Updated: October 2026

Maharashtra State Lottery ke afternoon draws usually ~4:00-4:15 PM ke beech hote hain, aur result draw ke kuch der baad official website lottery.maharashtra.gov.in par publish hota hai. Iske alawa Sagar Lakshmi jaise weekly schemes aur monthly draws ke apne schedule hote hain, jinhe official site par confirm karna chahiye.

Related Jankari

Draw Schedule Ek Nazar Me

Maharashtra me roz afternoon draws hote hain jinka time aam taur par 4:00 se 4:15 PM ke aas-paas hota hai. Draw ke baad winning numbers verify karke result list taiyaar hoti hai aur website par upload hoti hai — is process me thoda samay lag sakta hai, isliye draw time par turant result na mile to thoda wait karein.

Sagar Lakshmi jaise weekly schemes ka draw hafte ke fix din hota hai, jabki monthly draws mahine me ek baar hote hain. Inka exact schedule official notification me diya hota hai. Apne ticket par draw date zaroor check karein aur result bhi usi date ka dekhein.

Timing Me Badlav Ho Sakta Hai

Yahan di hui timing usual schedule hai — holidays, technical reasons ya official decisions se draw ka samay badal sakta hai. Kisi khaas din ka exact time jaanne ke liye official website ya authorized retailer se confirm kar lein. Weekly aur monthly schemes ka schedule bhi official notification se hi final hota hai.

Ticket kharidne ki cutoff timing draw time se pehle hoti hai — last moment par ticket lene ka wait na karein. Apne retailer se us din ki sale band hone ka time pehle hi pooch lein.

Draw Se Pehle Kya Dhyan Rakhein

Ticket hamesha authorized retailer se printed MRP par lein — Maharashtra me ticket price usually ~₹6-10 hota hai, exact amount ticket par printed MRP se confirm karein. Ticket par scheme ka naam, draw date aur series code verify kar lein taaki galat draw ka ticket na le baithein.

Practical tip:

Draw Ke Peeche Kya Hota Hai

Draw time par actually kya hota hai, ye jaanna bhi useful hai. Fix time par draw process se winning numbers nikale jaate hain, phir unhe verify kiya jaata hai aur official result list taiyaar hoti hai. Is verification aur publishing process me thoda samay lagta hai — isliye draw ke exact time par website refresh karne se result turant nahi milta, aur ye bilkul normal hai.

Is process ko samajhne ka fayda ye hai ki aap bechain nahi honge. Draw ke 15-30 minute baad check karna zyada practical hai. Aur hamesha official source par bharosa karein — social media ya WhatsApp forwards me aane wale "result" screenshots galat ya purane ho sakte hain, isliye lottery.maharashtra.gov.in hi final source hai.

Weekly scheme ka ticket lete waqt retailer se draw ka din confirm kar lein aur phone me reminder laga lein — kai log draw date bhool jaate hain aur claim window miss kar dete hain.
