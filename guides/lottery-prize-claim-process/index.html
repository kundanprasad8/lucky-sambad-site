Lottery Prize Claim Process — Step-by-Step Pura Tarika

Seedha Jawab: Lottery prize claim karne ke liye: (1) Original signed ticket surakshit rakhein, (2) ₹10,000 tak ke prizes authorized retailer/stockist se turant lein, (3) usse bade prizes ke liye lottery department me claim form + PAN + ID proof + bank details ke saath 30 din ke andar apply karein, (4) verification ke baad prize bank transfer se milta hai (30% TDS katkar).

Last Updated: October 2026 | By Lucky Sambad Editorial Team

Lottery jeetne ke baad sabse important step hai prize claim — aur yahin sabse zyada confusion hota hai. Kahan jana hai? Kya documents chahiye? Kitna time lagega? Tax kitna katega? Ye complete guide har prize slab ke liye exact process batayegi.

Slab 1: Chhote Prizes (₹10,000 Tak) — Sabse Aasan

  1. Retailer ke paas jayein: Kisi bhi authorized retailer ya stockist ke paas original ticket le jayein.
  2. Ticket verify karwayein: Retailer result sheet se number match karega.
  3. Paise lein: Verification ke baad turant cash mil jata hai.
  4. Ticket de dein: Retailer ticket rakh lega (usey department se reimbursement milta hai).
  5. Receipt lein: Agar badi raqam hai (₹5,000+) to receipt zaroor lein.

Note: ₹10,000 se kam par koi TDS nahi kat ta — poori raqam milti hai.

Slab 2 & 3: Bade Prizes — Department Claim Process

Step 1: Documents Taiyaar Karein

  • Original winning ticket — peeche naam + sign kiya hua
  • Claim form — lottery office se ya official website se
  • PAN card — ₹10,000+ prizes ke liye mandatory
  • Government ID proof — Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport
  • Address proof — Aadhaar / utility bill
  • Bank details — Cancelled cheque ya passbook copy (prize bank me aayega)
  • Passport photos — 2-3 copies

Step 2: Claim Form Bharein

  • Form me ticket number, draw date, prize amount sahi-sahi bharein.
  • Bank account details double-check karein — paise isi me aayega.
  • Form par sign karein aur date dalein.
  • Koi column khali na chhodein — incomplete form reject ho sakta hai.

Step 3: Office Me Jama Karein

  • Apne district lottery office ya directorate jayein.
  • Ticket + form + documents jama karein.
  • Acknowledgement receipt lena na bhoolein — ispar claim number hoga.
  • Receipt ko sambhal kar rakhein — ye aapke claim ka saboot hai.

Step 4: Verification Ka Intezaar Karein

  • Department ticket ki authenticity verify karega (2-6 hafte lag sakte hain).
  • Kabhi-kabhi additional documents mange jate hain — turant de dein.
  • Claim number se status follow up kar sakte hain.

Step 5: Payment Receive Karein

  • Verification ke baad prize bank transfer se aata hai.
  • TDS katkar amount aayega (₹10,000+ par 30% + cess).
  • TDS certificate (Form 16A) milega — tax filing me kaam aayega.

Claim Me Fraud Se Kaise Bachein?

  • "Claim karwa dunga, commission dunga": Anjaan agents ko ticket na dein. Khud department jayein.
  • "Pehle fees bhejo": Real claim me aapse pehle paise nahi mange jate.
  • Ticket ki photocopy se kaam: Koi kahe ki photocopy se claim ho jayega — jhooth hai.
  • Fake office: Sirf official address par jayein — website se verify karein.

Sources: Nagaland/Kerala State Lotteries claim rules, Income Tax Act Section 194B.

District Office Me Kya Hota Hai? (Inside View)

  1. Token/reception: Claim counter par jayein, purpose batayein.
  2. Document check: Officer sabhi documents verify karega — originals dekhega.
  3. Ticket verification: Ticket ki authenticity check hogi (UV light, serial number database).
  4. Form entry: Aapka claim system me register hoga — claim number milega.
  5. Acknowledgement: Receipt lein — ispar claim number aur date hogi.
  6. Follow-up: Claim number se status poochh sakte hain (phone ya visit).

Claim Status Kaise Track Karein?

  • Receipt sambhal kar rakhein — ispar claim number hai.
  • 2 hafte baad office me phone karke status poochhein.
  • Agar additional documents mange jayein to turant de dein — delay na karein.
  • Har visit ka record rakhein (date, kis se mile, kya kaha).

Kya Online Claim Ho Sakta Hai?

Filhal nahi. Adhikansh state lotteries me claim physical hi hota hai kyunki original ticket jama karna zaroori hai. Jo website "online claim" ka daava kare, wo fraud hai. Future me kuch states online system la sakti hain — official announcement ka intezaar karein.

Joint Winners Ya Group Ticket Ka Claim

Agar ticket group me kharida tha (doston ne milkar):

  • Pehle se agreement hona chahiye tha — ab jhagda hoga.
  • Claim ek vyakti ke naam par hoga — baad me aapas me baantein.
  • Tax liability claim karne wale par aayegi — iska hisaab pehle kar lein.
  • Likhit agreement banayein paise baantne se pehle.
  • Future ke liye: group ticket par sabke sign karwa lein pehle se.

Claim Process Me Madad Kahan Se Milegi?

  • Lottery office helpline: Official website par contact numbers hote hain.
  • Retailer: Chhote prizes ke liye retailer hi kaafi hai.
  • CA (Chartered Accountant): Bade prizes ke liye tax planning me madad.
  • Lawyer: Agar koi dispute ho to.
  • Lucky Sambad guides: Har step ke liye hamare detailed guides padhein.

Kis se madad NA lein: Anjaan "agents" jo commission par claim karwane ka daava karte hain — ye aksar fraud hote hain.

❓ Aksar Puche Jaane Wale Sawal

Lottery prize kaise claim karein?

₹10,000 tak retailer se turant, usse upar lottery department me ticket + PAN + ID + bank details ke saath 30 din me claim karein.

Prize claim ke liye kya documents chahiye?

Original ticket, PAN card, ID proof, address proof, bank details, claim form, photos.

Prize kab tak milega?

Chhote prizes turant, bade prizes verification ke baad 2-6 hafte me bank transfer se.

Kya claim ke liye paise dene hote hain?

Nahi. Real claim me aapse pehle koi fees nahi mangta. Mangne wala fraud hai.

TDS kitna katega?

₹10,000 se upar ~31.2% (30% + 4% cess). ₹10,000 tak zero.
