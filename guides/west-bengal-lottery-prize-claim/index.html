West Bengal Lottery Prize Claim Process

Seedha Jawab: West Bengal lottery ka prize draw date se 30 din ke andar claim karein. Ticket ke peeche sign karein, documents (ticket, Aadhaar/PAN, bank details) ke saath sahi office jayein. ₹10,000+ par 30% TDS katega.

West Bengal lottery me inaam jeetne par bahut-bahut badhai! Lekin inaam haasil karne ke liye sahi prakriya follow karna zaroori hai. West Bengal Lotteries Department ke niyamon ke anusaar draw date se 30 din ke andar claim karna anivarya hai — der hui to prize lapse ho jayega. 30 din ke andar; chhota prize retailer se, bada prize lottery office se.

Prize Claim Karne Ki Last Date Kya Hai?

Seedha jawab: draw ke din se 30 din. Ye deadline sakht hai — 31vein din claim accept nahi hoga. Isliye jeetne ke baad der na karein; pehle hafte me hi claim process shuru kar dein. Chhuttiyon ya office band hone ka bahana nahi chalega, isliye aakhri din ka intezar kabhi na karein.

Step-by-Step Claim Process

  1. Ticket par turant sign karein: Jeetne wale ticket ke peeche apna naam, sign aur date likh dein. Ye sabse pehla aur sabse zaroori kadam hai — taaki ticket gum ho ya chori ho to koi aur claim na kar sake.
  2. Ticket ko surakshit rakhein: Ticket ko laminate na karayein, stapler/pin na lagayein, aur paani/dhoop se bachayein. Kharab ya apaathya (unreadable) ticket reject ho sakta hai.
  3. Result verify karein: West Bengal result page aur official site (lotterysambad.com) dono par apna number milayein. Series + number dono match hone chahiye.
  4. Sahi office chunein: Neeche table dekhein — inaam ki rakam ke hisaab se kahan jana hai.
  5. Documents taiyaar karein: Original ticket, bhara hua claim form, ID/address proof, PAN (bade inaam par anivarya), bank details, 2 photo.
  6. Form jama karein: Office me form + documents jama karein aur receipt/acknowledgement zaroor lein.
  7. Verification ka intezar: Lottery vibhag ticket ki authenticity verify karta hai — isme kuch din se kuch hafte lag sakte hain.
  8. Payment payein: Verify hone par TDS katkar rakam aapke bank account me aati hai ya demand draft milta hai.

Tax Kitna Katega?

₹10,000 se zyada ke inaam par 30% TDS + 4% cess (kul 31.2%) source par hi kat jayega. Matlab ₹1 crore jeetne par lagbhag ₹68.8 lakh haath me aayenge. Poori ganana West Bengal lottery tax guide me dekhein.

Wo 5 Galtiyan Jo Prize Ganwa Deti Hain

  • 30 din ki deadline miss karna — sabse aam aur sabse badi wajah
  • Ticket kho dena, phaad dena ya laminate kara dena
  • Ticket par sign na karna — koi aur claim kar sakta hai
  • Kisi agent/dalal ko ticket dekar "main claim kara dunga" par bharosa karna
  • "Processing fee" ke naam par thagon ko paise de dena — asli me koi fee nahi lagti

Claim Status Kaise Track Karein?

Form jama karte waqt mili receipt/acknowledgement number sambhal kar rakhein. Isi se aap lottery office me apne claim ki sthiti pooch sakte hain. Bade claims me verification me 3–6 hafte lagna samanya hai — ghabrayein nahi, par receipt zaroor rakhein.

Claim Form Me Kya-Kya Bharna Hai?

Claim form me aam taur par ye details mangi jati hain — pehle se taiyaar rakhein taaki office ke chakkar na lagane padein:

  • Winner ka poora naam — Aadhaar/PAN jaisa hi (spelling match honi chahiye)
  • Ticket details — series, ticket number, draw date, scheme ka naam
  • Prize details — kaun sa prize (1st/2nd/3rd), rakam
  • Address — permanent aur correspondence dono
  • Bank details — account number, IFSC, bank/branch ka naam
  • Declaration — ki ticket aapne khud kharida aur aap hi winner hain

Verification Process Me Kya Hota Hai?

  1. Ticket authenticity check: Ticket ke security features (hologram, barcode, printing) verify hote hain
  2. Record matching: Ticket number lottery ke official record se milaya jata hai
  3. Duplicate claim check: Dekha jata hai ki isi ticket par pehle koi claim to nahi hua
  4. Document verification: Aapke ID aur bank details check hote hain
  5. Approval: Sab sahi mila to payment approve hota hai

Isi wajah se bade claims me 15–45 din lagte hain — ye suraksha ke liye hai, pareshani ke liye nahi.

❓ Aksar Puche Jaane Wale Sawal

West Bengal lottery prize claim ki last date kya hai?

Draw date se 30 din ke andar claim karna anivarya hai. 31vein din ke baad prize lapse ho jata hai aur koi claim accept nahi hota — isliye der na karein.

Prize claim ke liye kaun se documents chahiye?

Original signed ticket, bhara hua claim form, Aadhaar, PAN (₹10,000+ par anivarya), address proof, bank details (cancelled cheque) aur 2 passport photo.

Kya prize claim ke liye koi fee deni hoti hai?

Nahi, bilkul nahi. Asli lottery me claim ke liye kabhi advance/processing fee nahi mangi jati. Fee mangne wala 100% thag hai.

Chhota inaam (₹10,000 tak) kahan se milega?

Kisi bhi authorized retailer/agent se turant cash mil jata hai. Ticket dikhayein, paisa lein.

Bade inaam me kitna samay lagta hai?

Verification me aam taur par 15–45 din lagte hain. Receipt number se status track kar sakte hain.
