Bumper Lottery Kya Hota Hai? Complete Guide

Seedha Jawab: Bumper lottery festival ya special occasion par nikalne wali special lottery hai jisme first prize ₹5-25 crore tak hota hai (regular daily lottery me ₹1 crore). Ticket price ₹100-500 hota hai (regular ₹6-50 se mehnga). Ye saal me kuch baar hi aati hai — Diwali, Onam, Christmas, New Year jaise maukon par.

Last Updated: October 2026 | By Lucky Sambad Editorial Team

"Bumper lottery" sunte hi crores ka khayal aata hai — aur sahi bhi hai! Bumper lotteries me India ke sabse bade prizes hote hain. Lekin ticket mehnga hota hai, saal me kuch baar hi mauka milta hai, aur fraud bhi zyada hota hai. Ye complete guide bumper lottery ka A to Z samjhayegi.

Bumper Lottery Kya Hai? (Definition)

Bumper lottery special occasion par organize ki jane wali limited-time lottery hai:

  • Occasion: Diwali, Onam, Christmas, New Year, Pongal, Vishu jaise festivals.
  • Duration: Tickets 1-3 mahine pehle se bikte hain, draw festival ke aas-paas.
  • Prize: Regular se 5-25x zyada — crores me!
  • Tickets: Limited quantity — jaldi khatm hote hain.
  • Hype: Poora state (kabhi poora desh) khelta hai!

Bumper Ticket Kaise Kharidein? (Step-by-Step)

  1. Announcement ka intezaar karein: Official notification — newspaper ya website par.
  2. Trusted retailer chunein: Purana, authorized seller — bumper me fraud zyada hai.
  3. Advance me kharidein: 1-2 mahine pehle — baad me nakli tickets badh jate hain.
  4. Ticket verify karein: Bumper series ka naam, date, price — sab check karein.
  5. Price confirm karein: MRP se zyada na dein — black marketing hoti hai.
  6. Photo backup: Mehnga ticket hai — turant photo lein.
  7. Surakshit rakhein: Draw tak (1-3 mahine) sambhal kar rakhein!

Bumper Lottery Me Fraud — Extra Savdhaan!

  • Nakli bumper tickets: Mehnga ticket = zyada fraud. Print quality dhyan se check karein.
  • Black marketing: MRP se 2-3x me bechte hain — na kharidein.
  • "Confirmed" tickets: "Ye series jeetegi" — fraud hai.
  • Online bumper: WhatsApp par bumper tickets 100% nakli hain.
  • Old bumper tickets: Pichhle saal ke tickets bechne wale — expire ho chuke hain.

Bumper Result — Kab Aur Kaise Check Karein?
