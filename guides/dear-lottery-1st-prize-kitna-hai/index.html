Kerala me 1st prize scheme par depend karta hai

Kerala Lottery me har hafte alag-alag schemes ke draws hote hain, aur har scheme ka 1st prize alag hota hai. Weekly draws me 1st prize aam taur par ₹70-80 lakh tak hota hai, jabki bumper draws (jo festival ya khaas maukon par nikalte hain) me 1st prize crores me pahunch jata hai. Kerala ka ticket aam taur par ₹40 ka hota hai. Isliye Kerala ka ticket kharidte waqt scheme ke naam ke saath uska official prize structure zaroor check karein.

Kerala Kerala Lottery ke alag-alag weekly schemes ke results aur unke prize details aap hamare Kerala results page par dekh sakte hain. Har scheme ka draw din bhi fixed hota hai, isliye apne ticket par draw date milana na bhoolein.

1st prize par tax ka asar samajhiye

1st prize ki ghoshit rashi aur haath me aane wali rashi me farq hota hai, kyunki lottery winnings par tax lagta hai. ₹10,000 se zyada ki jeet par 30% TDS (Section 194B, Income Tax Act) kaat liya jata hai. Matlab Nagaland Dear Lottery ka ₹1 crore jeetne par lagbhag ₹30 lakh TDS katega, aur surcharge plus 4% health aur education cess bhi lag sakta hai — haath me lagbhag ₹69-70 lakh aayega. Tax ke baare me poori jaankari ke liye hamara tax wala FAQ zaroor padhein.

1st prize lage to turant kya karein

Agar aapka number 1st prize me aaya hai to sabse pehle ticket ko surakshit jagah rakhein aur uske peeche apna naam likhkar sign kar dein — taaki koi aur us par daava na kar sake. Phir draw ki date se 30 din ke andar prize claim karna hota hai, isliye der na karein. Claim ke liye original ticket, claim form, ID proof, address proof, PAN card, passport-size photos aur bank account details chahiye honge. Nagaland Nagaland Dear Lottery ke results aur claim se judi jaankari aap hamare Nagaland page par bhi dekh sakte hain.

Practical tip: Result dekhte hi ticket ki dono taraf se saaf photo le lein aur original ticket ko paani ya dhoop se door, kisi file me sambhal kar rakhein — claim ke liye original ticket hi kaam aata hai.
