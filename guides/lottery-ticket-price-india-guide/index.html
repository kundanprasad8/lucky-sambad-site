MRP — Aapka Adhikaar

  • Har ticket par MRP printed hoti hai — ye maximum price hai.
  • Retailer isse zyada nahi le sakta — ye kanoon hai.
  • Zyada mange to: Mana karein → Dusri dukaan try karein → Complaint karein (1915).
  • Discount: MRP se kam me mil sakta hai — lekin zyada kabhi nahi.

Price Badhne Ke Kaaran — Samjhiye

  • GST: Ticket price me tax included hota hai — rate badle to price badhega.
  • Prize increase: Bada prize = mehnga ticket (bumper).
  • Inflation: Saalon me price revise hote hain.
  • Scheme change: Nayi scheme me nayi pricing ho sakti hai.
