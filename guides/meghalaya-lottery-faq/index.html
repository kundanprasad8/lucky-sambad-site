Meghalaya lottery kya hai?

Meghalaya Lotteries Department dwara chalayi jane wali sarkari lottery hai. Isme Singham series jaise schemes hote hain aur ₹25 lakh se ₹1 crore tak tak ka inaam jeeta ja sakta hai.

Meghalaya lottery ticket price kitna hai?

Meghalaya lottery ticket kahan se kharidein?

Result Aur Draw Se Jude Sawal

Meghalaya lottery ka result kab aata hai?

Draw timing: Dopahar ka draw lagbhag 12:35 PM, Raat ka draw lagbhag 8:30 PM IST. Result draw ke turant baad Meghalaya result page par update hota hai.

Prize claim ki last date kya hai?

Prize Claim Aur Tax Se Jude Sawal

Meghalaya lottery me inaam kaise claim karein?

Ticket ke peeche sign karein aur draw date se 30 din ke andar claim karein. Chhota inaam retailer se, bada inaam lottery office se milta hai. Poori process claim guide me hai.

Lottery jeetne par kitna tax lagega?

31.2% (30% tax + 4% cess). ₹10,000 se zyada par TDS source par kat jata hai. Koi deduction nahi milti.

Ticket kho jaye to kya hoga?

Bina original ticket ke prize claim nahi hota. Ticket sambhal kar rakhein, backup photo lein.

Suraksha Se Jude Sawal

Kya online lottery ticket kharid sakte hain?

Nahi, isse bachein. State lotteries paper ticket par chalti hain. Online bechne wali zyadatar sites farzi hoti hain.

'Sure win number' bechne wale sahi hain?

Nahi, 100% thag hain. Lottery me koi number predict nahi kar sakta. Aise logon ko paise kabhi na dein.

Kya Meghalaya lottery me umar ki seema hai?

Haan — ticket kharidne ke liye kam se kam 18 saal ki umar zaroori hai.

Aur Aam Sawal

Kya Meghalaya lottery se ameer ban sakte hain?

Imandari se kahein to chances bahut kam hain — lottery poori tarah luck par based hai. Ise manoranjan samjhein, kamai ka zariya nahi. Budget me khelein.

Ticket par GST lagta hai?

Nahi, alag se nahi. Ticket ki MRP me sab kuch shamil hai — retailer MRP se zyada ya alag se GST nahi maang sakta.

Kya dusre rajya ka Meghalaya lottery ticket le sakte hain?

Ticket usi rajya me kharidna padta hai jahan lottery authorized hai. Meghalaya ka ticket Meghalaya ke authorized retailers se hi lein.

Meghalaya lottery me ek se zyada ticket le sakte hain?

Haan, le sakte hain — par budget me rahein. Zyada ticket ka matlab zyada kharcha bhi hai. Jeet ki guarantee kabhi nahi hoti.
