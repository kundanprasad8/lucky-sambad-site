1st Prize (₹21 Lakh): The jackpot — awarded to the single ticket matching the complete winning number including the series letter. This is the life-changing prize that makes headlines, and it is subject to 30% TDS deduction before payout.

Consolation Prize (₹1,000): A special tier for tickets that match the 1st prize number in every digit except the series letter. If the jackpot is 63B 56525, then 63A 56525, 63C 56525 and so on each win ₹1,000. It softens the sting of a near-miss.

2nd Prize (₹9,000): Awarded to tickets matching a separate set of drawn numbers — typically around ten winning numbers per draw. A meaningful win that many players aim for.
