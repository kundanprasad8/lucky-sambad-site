📰 Dhan Kesari vs Lottery Sambad — Same Thing?

Yes, they are the same. Dhan Kesari is a well-known Hindi newspaper (founded 1992) that has printed lottery results for decades — so widely read that "Dhan Kesari" became shorthand for the Lottery Sambad results themselves. When someone searches "Dhan Kesari lottery result", they want the 1 PM, 6 PM or 8 PM Lottery Sambad winning numbers — exactly what this page shows.

The draws, prize structure, ticket prices and official sources are identical. Only the name differs.
