🎰 How Lottery Draws Work

Ever wondered what happens between buying a ticket and seeing your number on this page? Here is the full process:

  1. Ticket purchase: you buy a pre-printed ticket from an authorized retailer. Each ticket carries a unique number — there is no choosing your own numbers in Indian government lotteries.
  2. The draw: at the scheduled time, officials conduct the draw using mechanical draw machines — transparent chambers with numbered balls, drawn one by one in full public view. Many draws are live-streamed or recorded.
  3. Official supervision: a panel of government-nominated judges oversees every draw to ensure fairness. The results are signed off before publication.
  4. PDF publication: the state lottery department publishes an official result PDF listing every winning number across all prize tiers. This is the legal record of the draw.
  5. Verification & listing: Lucky Sambad's pipeline fetches these PDFs (or trusted secondary sources), extracts the winning numbers, marks them Verified, and publishes them here — usually within minutes.

The entire chain is designed so that no single person can predict or alter the outcome. If anyone promises you "guaranteed winning numbers", it is a scam.

💸 Lottery Winnings & Tax in India

Winning is exciting — but India taxes lottery income heavily. Here is what every winner must know:

  • 30% TDS above ₹10,000: any prize exceeding ₹10,000 attracts 30% tax deducted at source (plus 4% health & education cess, effectively ~31.2%) before the money reaches you. The lottery department deducts it automatically.
  • Report in your ITR: lottery winnings are taxed as "income from other sources" at a flat 30% (+ cess), with no deductions or exemptions allowed. Declare the gross amount in your Income Tax Return for the assessment year.
  • 30-day claim deadline: you must claim within 30 days of the draw date, or the prize lapses to the government. Do not wait.
  • Documents needed: original winning ticket, government ID proof, address proof, passport-size photos, a filled claim form, and your PAN card (mandatory for TDS credit).
  • Keep records: save the TDS certificate (Form 16A) issued by the lottery department — you will need it when filing returns.

Disclaimer: this is general information, not tax advice. Consult a chartered accountant for your specific situation.
