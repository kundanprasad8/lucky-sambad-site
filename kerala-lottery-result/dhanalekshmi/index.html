🏆 Claiming Your Dhanalekshmi Prize — Step by Step

  1. Confirm the number: check the Dhanalekshmi prize table above row by row, then the official PDF. Pay special attention to the series letter — a wrong series means a different prize (or none).
  2. Up to ₹10,000: any authorized Kerala retailer pays immediately against the original ticket.
  3. Above ₹10,000: the Kerala lottery office handles it. Carry the original ticket, PAN card, Aadhaar, address proof, passport photos and a filled claim form; the office verifies the ticket before releasing the prize.
  4. Deadline: 30 days from the draw date. The Dhanalekshmi claim window does not extend for weekends, holidays or travel — claim the same week you discover the win.
  5. Tax: 30% TDS (+ cess) is deducted from prizes above ₹10,000. The net amount is what lands in your account; the TDS certificate (Form 16A) is your proof for the tax return.
  6. After claiming: keep the receipt and the TDS certificate together. If the prize was large, consider speaking to a tax advisor before spending it — lottery income is fully taxable.
