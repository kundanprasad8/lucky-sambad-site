💰 Labhlaxmi Power Prize Tiers Explained

The Labhlaxmi Power draw distributes prizes across multiple tiers so that winning is not an all-or-nothing affair:

Jackpot (1st Prize, ₹0.1 Lakh): A single ticket takes the headline prize each draw. The number includes a series letter (like 63B 56525) — your ticket must match it exactly, series included.

Consolation (₹1,000): Unique to Indian state lotteries — if your number matches the jackpot digits but carries a different series letter, you still win ₹1,000. Always check this tier; it is the most commonly overlooked win.

2nd Prize (₹9,000): Ten winning numbers per draw. This tier turns a ₹10 ticket into a serious payout.

3rd (₹450), 4th (₹250), 5th (₹120) Prizes: These tiers are drawn from shorter digit matches, which is why they produce the most winners. The 5th prize's four-digit match means roughly 1 in 2,500 tickets wins something at this level.

Prize amounts are fixed by the Sikkim lottery department and identical for every Labhlaxmi Power draw. Winnings up to ₹10,000 can be claimed at any authorized retailer; larger prizes require a visit to the lottery office within 30 days. TDS of 30% applies to prizes above ₹10,000.
